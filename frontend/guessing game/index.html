How to Play Number Guesser

1. The computer selects a secret whole number inside the selected difficulty range.

2. Enter your guess. The Thermal Radar tells you if you are boiling hot, warm, cold, or freezing!

3. The Range Slider automatically narrows down the lowest and highest possible bounds.

4. Use the Midpoint Hint to play mathematically optimal Binary Search!
