How to Play Hangman Quest

1. Guess the hidden word one letter at a time.

2. You have 6 lives. Each incorrect letter adds another piece to the gallows.

3. You can click the virtual on-screen letters or type directly on your physical keyboard.

4. Use the Reveal Clue or Reveal Vowel buttons when you're stuck!
