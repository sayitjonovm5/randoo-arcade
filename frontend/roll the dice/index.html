How to Play 3D Dice Simulator

1. Choose anywhere from 1 to 6 dice to roll simultaneously.

2. Tap Roll Dice or press the Spacebar to roll with 3D physical rotation!

3. Switch to High-Low Predictor mode to test your probability intuition: guess whether the next sum will be Higher, Lower, or Equal to build a win streak!
