How to Play Tic-Tac-Toe Neon

1. Players take turns placing their mark (X or O) on an empty grid cell.

2. The first player to align 3 matching marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins!

3. Master AI implements recursive Minimax search and plays mathematically optimal moves.

4. Switch to Tactical AI or 2 Players Local to play with friends!
